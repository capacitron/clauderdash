07 · Principles

What This Dashboard Is

Forensic diary
Records what happened. Doesn't judge. Surfaces patterns you didn't notice yourself.
Numbers tell stories
Every metric has a sentence. "55.6%" is not enough — "2.8× over target" tells you something.
Live, not weekly
Recommendations load fresh on every visit. No cron. No opt-in. Just open the dashboard.
Local only
127.0.0.1. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry. Your data stays on your machine.
